# Login & Access Instructions - Aligarh Cyber Wednesday Portal

## 🌐 Portal Link
- Local Access: `http://localhost:8080`

---

## 👥 1. Client Access (Police Stations / Field Officers)
- **Password**: None required (Open Access)
- **How to Submit Data**:
  1. Open `http://localhost:8080`
  2. Click **New Record** button in the Wednesday Log section.
  3. Select Aligarh Police Station, enter campaign place, attendee count, officer name, date, and upload photo.
  4. Click **Submit Record**.
