Aligarh Cyber Crime Cell Prevention Rules

Key awareness instructions distributed by Aligarh Cyber Crime Cell every Wednesday to protect citizens against online fraud.

Digital Arrest & Fake Police Calls

Scammers impersonate police or CBI via video calls alleging parcel seizure or arrest warrants.

Aligarh Cyber Crime Cell Tip: Real police never arrest anyone on video calls. Call 1930 / +91 7839876377 immediately.

Banking & OTP Frauds

Fraudulent calls requesting debit card OTP, PIN, or installation of remote access apps (AnyDesk, QuickSupport).

Aligarh Cyber Crime Cell Tip: Never share your 6-digit OTP or UPI PIN with anyone.

Fake Stock & Work-from-Home Scams

Telegram groups promising guaranteed daily stock earnings or paid YouTube rating tasks.

Aligarh Cyber Crime Cell Tip: Never transfer money into unverified personal bank accounts for job tasks.

AI Voice Cloning & Emergency Fraud

AI-generated calls imitating a family member's voice claiming they met with an accident in Aligarh.

Aligarh Cyber Crime Cell Tip: Disconnect immediately and call your family member directly.
