CITIZEN ADVISORY

Cyber Security Safety Rules

Financial Safety

Never share OTP, PIN, Password, or CVV with anyone calling from unknown numbers claiming to be bank officials.

Suspicious Links

Do not click on unverified links received via SMS, WhatsApp, or Email promising lottery, jobs, or instant loans.

Report Immediately

If defrauded, immediately dial 1930 or register a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in within golden hours.
